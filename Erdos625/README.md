## Analytical wrapper boundary

The root module also imports `AnalyticalWrapper.lean`, which exposes
`Problem625.Analytical.erdos_625_full_analytical` as a Lean-facing
dependency-closure interface for the full analytical route.  Not Lean-certified: the analytical wrapper theorem `erdos_625_full_analytical` depends on three WHP bridge obligations not yet formalized in Lean (recorded in
`../proof/ANALYTICAL_WRAPPER_AXIOM_SNAPSHOT.txt`).

Current source-boundary caveats: the middle source artifact covers only
`x <= 1-epsilon_0` for fixed `epsilon_0 > 0`; the residual region
`x > 1-epsilon_0` is handled by the separate upper branch (Regime III).
The upper source artifact's explicit directed interval tables are now
supplied in `proof/source-notes/upper-boundary-r2-explicit-interval-tables-2026-05-13.md`.

## What `#print axioms` shows

```lean
import Erdos625.PublishableProof
#print axioms Problem625.Publishable.erdos_625_full_clean
```

returns exactly **7 entries**:

- **4 paper-backed axioms**: two literal HP-2023 citations
  (`partB_alphaMinusTwo_firstMomentBelowOne_source`,
  `chi_alphaMinusTwo_lower_bound_whp`) and two axioms that are **not**
  literal one-citation paper quotes — `lemma_7_20_modified` (HYBRID:
  HP-2023 Lemma 7.20 + our in-repository, **not peer-reviewed**
  numerical certificate `lemma_7_10_ext`) and
  `zeta_alphaMinusTwo_upper_bound_whp` (EXTRAPOLATION:
  $\alpha-1\to\alpha-2$ symmetric version of Heckel 2024 Prop 5(b),
  **not literally in print**). See
  [`../paper/SOURCES.md`](../paper/SOURCES.md) and the dedicated audit
  notes in [`../paper/SOURCES.md`](../paper/SOURCES.md).
- **3 Lean kernel axioms**: `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`.

**0 `sorryAx` on the proof path of `erdos_625_full_clean`.** The former non-reachable helper placeholders in `PartBProfileBridge.lean`,
`ChromaticConnection.lean`, and `ZetaConcentration.lean` have been converted
to named source seams tracked by source-axiom snapshots; they are not local
proof placeholders.

`grep "^axiom" Erdos625/*.lean` will show additional `axiom` declarations
that are **not** reachable from `erdos_625_full_clean` (they belong to
alternative chains or legacy routes); see `../paper/SOURCES.md`.

## Architecture: how the four theorems compose

```
erdos_625              (uses 3 axioms reachable from PartBProfileBridge + ZetaConcentration)
  ↓ extend by replacing one axiom
erdos_625_97           (uses lemma_7_20_modified; 1 paper axiom)
  ↓ case-split on InMainRangeMod
erdos_625_full         (good-case via erdos_625_97; crossing-case via 3 α-2 axioms)
  ↓ clean rpow comparison via rpow_clean_bound_eventually
erdos_625_full_clean   (flagship; same 4 axioms total)
```

`#print axioms` for the flagship `erdos_625_full_clean` includes the
crossing-case axioms (which `erdos_625` does not need) and
`lemma_7_20_modified` (which `erdos_625_97` introduced), but does **not**
transitively include the three `erdos_625` axioms (`heckel_offdiag_term_bound`
and the two `paperPartBExact*` axioms) because the flagship's good-case
branch goes through `erdos_625_97`, not `erdos_625`.
